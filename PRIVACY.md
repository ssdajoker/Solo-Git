# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is collected, used, and shared when you use Solo Git.

## Information We Collect

When you use Solo Git, we may collect the following information:

*   **Configuration Information**: We may collect information about your Solo Git configuration, such as the AI models you use and your budget settings. This information is stored locally on your machine and is not transmitted to us.
*   **Usage Information**: We may collect information about how you use Solo Git, such as the commands you run and the features you use. This information is anonymized and used to improve the product.
*   **API Keys**: Your Abacus.ai API key is stored locally on your machine and is only used to make API requests to the Abacus.ai service. We do not have access to your API key.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

*   Provide, maintain, and improve Solo Git.
*   Develop new features.
*   Understand how our users use Solo Git.

## Sharing Your Information

We do not share your personal information with third parties.

## Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. If you would like to exercise these rights, please contact us.

## Changes

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new privacy policy on this page.

## Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
